How I work

Turning ambiguity into structure

I take messy, ambiguous problems and give them structure, using a wide range of tools for scoping and prioritization to turn confusion into a clear plan.

Rapid AI-assisted prototyping

I use tools like Claude, Cursor, and Lovable to move from concept to interactive prototype fast, so we validate ideas before committing engineering time.

Cross-functional workshopping

I lead workshops that get cross-functional teams and leadership aligned on complex problems, rather than defending one idea alone.

Combining business, user, and technical needs

I care about the overlap between what people need, what the business needs, and what's actually feasible to build – that's where good products work.

Data-driven approach

I analyze user behavior patterns and conversion data myself, using tools like Amplitude and Google Analytics to see past the design and into what's actually happening.

Research-led, not research-heavy

I run interviews, usability tests, and session recordings myself, and know when a quick guerrilla test beats a two-week study.
